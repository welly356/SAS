About Us

We serve. We show up.

A grassroots Nepali initiative built on action, not words

SAS Art Of Giving is a volunteer-driven community that goes out into Nepal's most vulnerable communities and delivers real, hands-on service. We feed families, support children's education, provide healthcare essentials, and stand alongside those who have been forgotten.


Born on Facebook, built on compassion. We share every act of service publicly so our community can witness the journey — and be part of it.

We do not accept goods, items, or physical donations of any kind. Money only. This keeps our process clean, fair, and fully trackable.
